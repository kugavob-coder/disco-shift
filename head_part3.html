DISCO SHIFT

The morning after. The song is broken.
Shift it back, level by level.

Two buttons only: Left Shift / Right Shift
or the arrow keys ← / →,
or tap the left / right side of the screen.

Sticky Keys popping up? Use the arrow keys.

HOW TO PLAY

  • 1TUNE IT UP OR DOWNThe gold outline around the wave is out of shape. Tap to make it narrower / wider, hold to make it flatter / taller, until it hugs the wave.
  • 2MAKE IT FASTER OR SLOWERThe song keeps slipping too fast or too slow. Tap left / right to keep the needle in the gold zone as long as you can.
  • 3LINE UP THE COLORSFour spotlights split her into four colors. Tap to move a color left / right, hold to move it back / front, until it sits on the thin gold outline.

HOW TO PLAY

  • 4MORNING OR NIGHT? CATCH THE LIGHTLights fly in from the sides. Be in their world (left ☀, right ☾) when they reach her feet.
  • 5STRAIGHTEN THE VIEWThe world is tilted and she is out of shape. Tap to turn it left / right, hold to make her shorter / taller, until she fits the gold outline.
  • 6MOVE WHERE SHE POINTSPress the side she points to and bring her to the middle. Watch out, she shifts sides!

Then dance, and hear the song
the way you fixed it.
